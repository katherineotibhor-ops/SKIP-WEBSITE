Services

Five connected service areas covering credit, funding and the preparation that sits between them.

Service 01

Credit Consulting & Profile Improvement

We begin with a comprehensive review of your credit reports and overall financial profile. We identify inaccurate, incomplete or unverifiable information and develop a structured strategy addressing disputes, utilization, account mix, reporting history and funding readiness.

  • Full three-bureau report review
  • Identification of inaccurate, incomplete or unverifiable items
  • Structured dispute process, managed on your behalf
  • Utilization, account mix and reporting-history strategy
  • Progress updates as items are resolved

Where it is relevant to your situation, the review also extends beyond the three main bureaus:

  • Secondary consumer-reporting agencies
  • Banking-history consulting
  • ChexSystems and Early Warning Services review
  • Personal-information and employment-history review
  • Funding readiness and application sequencing

Accurate, current and verifiable information cannot be removed from a credit report, and you are entitled to dispute inaccurate information yourself at no cost. We will tell you plainly which items fall into which category.
