Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026. This is a working draft prepared for the client and has not been reviewed by a qualified attorney. It must be reviewed before launch.

1. Who we are

Elevated Identities ("we", "us", "our") is a credit and funding consulting company headquartered in South Carolina, USA, providing credit consulting, credit-profile improvement, funding-readiness guidance and authorized tradeline services. You can reach us at Elevatedid1@gmail.com or (602) 824-8796.

2. Information we collect

Through this website, we collect only the information you choose to submit through our enquiry form:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • The service you are interested in
  • The content of your message

If you go on to become a client, we will collect further information necessary to provide the service, which may include credit report data and identity documentation. That collection is governed by the service agreement you sign, not by this website policy alone.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a consultation
  • To provide the services you request
  • To keep records required for our business and legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Form processing

Enquiries submitted through this website are delivered to us by a third-party form processing service. That provider handles the message in transit and is bound by its own privacy terms. No enquiry data is stored on this website itself.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies and does not run third-party analytics. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the request necessary to serve those font files.

6. How we protect information

Credit-related information is sensitive, and we handle it accordingly. Access is limited to those who need it to deliver your service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. How long we keep information

Enquiries that do not become engagements are retained only as long as needed to respond and to keep ordinary business records. Client records are retained for the period required by our agreements and applicable law.

8. Your rights

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to retain it. Write to Elevatedid1@gmail.com and we will respond.

Separately, and regardless of this policy, you have the right under federal law to dispute inaccurate information on your credit report directly with the credit bureaus yourself, at no cost.

9. Children

This website and our services are not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Elevatedid1@gmail.com.
