How it works

A clear process, start to finish

Step 01

Free consultation

We talk through your goals, your timeline and where your credit and funding position stands right now.

Step 02

Review & plan

We review your reports and profile in detail, then set out a plan with the steps, sequence and realistic expectations.

Step 03

Execute & update

We carry out the agreed work and keep you updated as items progress, so you always know what stage you are at.

Your consultation

What actually happens on the first call

The consultation is a conversation, not a sales pitch. We ask what you are working towards — a mortgage, a business line of credit, a vehicle, or simply a stronger profile — and what your timeline looks like.

  • You tell us your goal and the timeline you are working to
  • We talk through what is currently on your reports and how lenders read it
  • We explain which parts of your profile can realistically change, and which cannot
  • We set out the likely sequence of work and what it involves
  • You decide whether to go ahead — there is no obligation and no cost

You will leave the call with a clear read on your position, whether or not you choose to work with us.

Why clients work with us

Structured, transparent and handled with care

A defined process

Every engagement follows the same structure: review, plan, execute, update. You always know which stage you are at and what happens next.

Nationwide service

Headquartered in South Carolina and working with clients across the United States. Consultations are held by phone, so location is not a barrier.

Confidential by default

Your reports and financial details are handled as confidential and used only to carry out the work you have asked for. See our Privacy Policy.

Realistic expectations

We tell you what a credit profile can and cannot do, and we do not promise scores or approvals. Accurate negative information cannot be removed.

Credit and funding together

Profile work and funding readiness are handled as one plan, so you are not improving a profile without knowing what it is being prepared for.

You keep your rights

You may dispute inaccurate information on your own credit report yourself, at no cost. We explain that openly rather than working around it.
